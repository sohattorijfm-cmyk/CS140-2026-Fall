What is JavaScript? How did it get its name?

JavaScript is a high-level programming language primarily used to make websites interactive and dynamic. JavaScript was created in 1995 by Brendan Eich at Netscape in just 10 days. Originally called Mocha and then LiveScript, it was renamed **JavaScript** as a marketing move to capitalize on the popularity of Sun Microsystems' Java—even though the two languages are completely unrelated.

How does your code prevent the form from being submitted? Why do we need to prevent it?

In JavaScript, you use the `event.preventDefault()` method to prevent the default form submission behavior (such as page reloading or sending data to a server). We prevent the default form submission primarily to stop the browser from automatically reloading or navigating away from the current page.
