Shohei Ohtani Interest

Shoei Ohtani currently plays on Dodgers since 2024. He started his career as a proffetional baseball player on Hokkaido Nippon Ham Fighters in Japanese proffetional baseball, called NPB. He has played there for 5 years and one of years, he became the MVP of the year and his team became the champion in NPB. In 2017, he started to play on Los Angeles Angels in MLB. Then, He has been playing on the Los Angeles Dodgers since the 2024 season.
