What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

positives

  • Free storage and bandwidth
  • Adaptive streaming & cross-device support
  • Built-in player & discoverability

negatives

  • Less visual & branding control
  • Ads and distractions
  • Platform dependency

What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

design tools, video editing effects, and data/machine learning operations

What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

transform changes how an element looks or sits on the page (moving, scaling, rotating, or skewing it), while transition makes property changes happen smoothly over time instead of snapping instantly.
