What is BYU?

BYU (Brigham Young University) is one of universities in the U.S. It is owned and operated by The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and is widely recognized for its high academic standards, affordable tuition, and unique campus culture. BYU has three campuses, in Utah which is the main and biggest campus, in Idaho, and in Hawaii. BYU also provides BYU Pathway Worldwide which is online educational system.

BYU provides so many programs. In particular, the business school (Marriott School of Business) and programs in fields such as accounting, IT, and computer science are highly regarded nationwide, with a strong track record of graduates securing positions at major corporations. Especially, Accounting program in BYU is one of the top programs in accounting programs in the U.S.
