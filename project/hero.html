Jackie Robinson

The Los Angeles Dodgers are one of the most popular teams in MLB, with a longer history than most other franchises. A notable feature of this team is its willingness to try new things. For example, they signed Jackie Robinson, who became the first African American to play in modern Major League Baseball at a time when racial prejudice was widespread in the U.S. Through the team's and his own dedicated efforts, people's attitudes began to change. Today, MLB features many players from diverse backgrounds, including Japan, Venezuela, and Puerto Rico.

Japanese Players

Many top Japanese baseball players have played for the Dodgers, including Hideo Nomo—the pioneer of modern Japanese players in MLB—Kenta Maeda, and Yu Darvish. Currently, there are three Japanese players on the team: Roki Sasaki, Yoshinobu Yamamoto, and Shohei Ohtani. Shohei Ohtani, in particular, is considered one of the best players in the world. He excels as both a pitcher and a hitter, playing a two-way role in games. This unique ability makes him one of the greatest players in the long history of MLB.
