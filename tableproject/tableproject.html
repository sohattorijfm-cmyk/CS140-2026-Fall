Background Information

Global Japanese communities have evolved significantly over the past century. While earlier migrations—particularly to North and South America—were driven by permanent settlement and agricultural work, modern diaspora populations in regions like Europe, Asia, and Africa are largely shaped by corporate globalization, international trade, and higher education. Today, major global hubs such as London, Shanghai, and Los Angeles serve as vital centers where expatriates and long-term residents maintain cultural ties while contributing to international business and diplomacy.
